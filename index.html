Escape the Bungalow 3D

Sanskriti Shukla is trapped on the top floor of the old bungalow. As Ujjwal, find the golden key, unlock her room and bring her out alive.

Drag anywhere to move. ⚡ Dash is a fast burst, ✦ Radiance freezes nearby ghosts, 👻 Veil makes you invisible. Power orbs 🔮 recharge everything. Keyboard: WASD or arrows, then 1, 2, 3.
